ArenaPlus (DigiPlus) · 2026 · Python, implicit, scikit-learn, MaxCompute SQL

Which league will a player bet next?

Question
Recommend sports leagues to active sportsbook players from twelve months of bets across two sportsbook vendors, and use the same scores to pick promotion targets.
What I built
Implicit-feedback ALS (library and a from-scratch NumPy solver), four baselines including same-season-last-year popularity, a time-split evaluation with a one-month gap, NDCG@K and paired bootstrap intervals. I mapped every match to its league through the vendor API and reconciled the extract to the warehouse within 1% for 25 of 26 vendor-months.
Result
Twelve-month popularity beat ALS (NDCG@10 0.618 vs 0.402). The training window ended in the NBA off-season, and ALS ranks a player's taste share, not their betting volume. Blending ALS with each player's history kept most of the accuracy and widened catalogue coverage from 0.1% to 16%.
Next
A count objective (Poisson factorization or BPR) and a league-by-month season prior. I pre-registered predictions before training; I got 0 of 11 right, which is why I write them down.

ArenaPlus (DigiPlus) · 2026 · scikit-learn gradient boosting, logistic regression

Who among the high-value players stops next month?

Question
Flag players with large recent volume who will place no bet in the next 30 days, early enough for a retention offer.
What I built
Fifteen monthly snapshots with 30 behavioral features from bets, deposits and withdrawals; trained on earlier months, tuned on August, tested once on September. Compared against the rule the CRM team already used: days since the last bet.
Result
AUC 0.91 and 5.8× lift in the top decile, which holds 58% of next month's churners. The simple recency rule was already strong (AUC 0.88); the model's gain is real but modest, +11% PR-AUC. Probabilities are well calibrated, and results held when I removed the two months hit by the August 2025 e-wallet regulation shock.

ArenaPlus (DigiPlus) · 2025–2026 · difference-in-differences, holdouts, MaxCompute SQL

Measuring CRM campaigns without fooling ourselves

Question
Campaign reports compared players who redeemed a bonus with players who did not, which credits the bonus with whatever made those players active in the first place.
What I built
A 10% randomized holdout for every lifecycle campaign, stratified by turnover, GGR and deposits; and for older campaigns, a triple-difference design that uses a product the bonus cannot touch as a placebo.
Result
Engagement selection explained 79% of the naive lift; the corrected return was 1.34× instead of 2.98×. The churn-prevention offer worked only where risk was high, and the effect fell to zero as activity rose. The redesigned program became the standard way the team reads every lifecycle campaign.

ArenaPlus × LiveMatch · 2026 · funnel sizing, scenario analysis

Should the live-score app carry its own betslip?

Question
Industry reports on in-stream and embedded betting suggested that removing the hop from our live-score app to the sportsbook would lift conversion. I proposed it and sized it before anyone built it.
What I built
The current funnel from deep-link clicks to a bet within a day, and three scenarios taken from published cases: +5–10% (betslip redesign), +30–40% (fewer app switches), and 2× as a ceiling (streaming studies with self-selection).
Result
About a third of clickers already bet within a day. Even the base case adds little in absolute terms, because deep-link clicks fell by about three quarters from June to September. Traffic was the constraint, not friction. I recommended a split test on match pages and a traffic fix before engineering work.

NSUSLAB (GGPoker) · 2023–2025 · Optimove, Snowflake, Tableau, SQL

Personalized win-back for a global poker room

Question
Blanket reactivation offers paid players who were coming back anyway and attracted bonus hunters.
What I built
Segments by inactivity, lifetime value, preferred format (tournaments, cash, Spin & Gold) and region, launched and A/B tested in Optimove; a daily cherry-picker screen with three rules (cost-to-value ratio, post-campaign activity, shared IPs); reporting moved to Optimove Datashare, Snowflake and Tableau.
Result
The pilot personalized reactivation returned 3.1× its cost. CRM-driven GGR grew 25% year over year. Players with a past big win returned at a higher rate, which became its own win-back segment. Automated reporting removed four days of manual work per cycle.

Korea Institute for Defense Analyses · 2020–2023 · text mining, RNN

Does state media signal a provocation in advance?

Question
Whether the language of North Korea's official newspaper changes in the week before military provocations.
What I built
A two-stage text-mining pipeline over the full text of Rodong Sinmun, focused on the seven days before events such as the sinking of the ROKS Cheonan and the shelling of Yeonpyeong Island; a provocation vocabulary checked with subject-matter experts; and an RNN that models word order as well as word frequency.
Result
80% accuracy on a held-out validation set, and evidence that official language carries observable signals before military action. Earlier text-forecasting work at TMAX BI was published in an SSCI journal.

How I work

Agents in the loop, tests in front

I use AI coding agents daily to write pipelines test-first and to run an independent code review before results go out. On the recommender, that review caught a deduplication bug before publication.

Reconcile before modeling

Every extract is checked against the warehouse totals the business already trusts. I report where the numbers disagree and why, before I report anything else.

Say what I expect first

I write predictions down before a model runs and score them afterwards. It keeps the analysis honest and shows where my intuition about the business is wrong.
